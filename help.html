Help & Walkthrough

Hints

Not sure what key goes where, or which NPCs need what items? Here are some hints for what you can use in different places.

Items ⇩

  • Brass Key - opens the garden doors in the cafe or the blue corridor
  • Iron Key - opens the hidden store behind the shelves in the cleaners' cupboard
  • Small Key - fits a very small keyhole in the white room, you'll need a friend with a good nose to find it
  • Lever - part of a mechanism in the observatory, reveals a secret
  • Bookshelf - an NPC with a lot of books might need this, try placing it near one of them
  • Birdhouse - belongs outside, where the birds can nest in it
  • Green Gem - part of an advanced machine hidden in the Complex
  • Battery - another part of the machine the green gem fits into

NPCs ⇩

  • Caretaker - needs his toolbox back before he can do much, check the workshop and yard to find it
  • Puppy - likes to know people are fun to play with before following them, give him a toy to show you're a friend
  • Scientist - fascinated by snowglobes, fossils, and anything strange and technical
  • Barista - she's happy to chat with anyone, makes a great cup of coffee, and loves flowers

Walkthrough

Really, truly stuck? Expand the sections below for either a simple or full walkthrough. Simple will only take you through the basic steps to find the exit, full will guide you to find all the secrets and unlock all the rooms before leaving.

Simple Walkthrough ⇩

** White Room **

- jump to reveal the first exit

- go east into the fossil exhibit

** Fossil Exhibit **

- go east into the gift shop

** Gift Shop **

- take a dog toy

- go west back into the fossil exhibit

** Fossil Exhibit **

- go north into the red corridor

** Red Corridor **

- go north into the art gallery

** Art Gallery **

- go west into the workshop

** Workshop **

- build a cart

- go west into the yard

** Yard **

- give the puppy the dog toy (he'll start following you)

- take the toolbox

- go east back into the workshop

** Workshop **

- go east back to the art gallery

** Art Gallery **

- go east into the blue corridor

** Blue Corridor **

- meet the caretaker

- give the caretaker his toolbox (you get a lever in exchange)

- go north into the observatory

** Observatory **

- use the lever

- go south west into the secret lab

** Secret Lab **

- take the brass key

- go east back to the blue corridor

** Blue Corridor **

- use the brass key to unlock the garden door

- go south east into the garden

** Garden **

- tell the puppy to dig (gives you the iron key)

- go west back to the blue corridor

** Blue Corridor **

- go south west back to the art gallery

** Art Gallery **

- go south back to the red corridor

** Red Corridor **

- go east into the cleaners' store

** Cleaners' Store **

- move the shelves

- use the iron key to unlock the hidden door

- go east into the hidden store

** Hidden Store **

- take the small key

- go west back to the cleaners' store

** Cleaners' Store **

- go west back to the red corridor

** Red Corridor **

- go south back to the fossil exhibit

** Fossil Exhibit **

- go west back to the white room

** White Room **

- sit on the bench (reveals a hidden keyhole)

- use the small key to unlock the hidden keyhole (opens the exit)

- go north into the glass corridor

** Glass Corridor **

- go west out of the exit door

- win the game!
